Northwind Offshore Wind Farm

The Northwind Offshore Wind Farm is a 600 megawatt project located 25 kilometres off the coast of Aurora Bay. It is developed by Tidewater Energy, a renewable developer headquartered in Port Linden, under a 20-year power purchase agreement with Aurora Bay Power and Light.

Tidewater Energy's chief executive, James Okafor, signed the agreement with Mayor Elena Vasquez in October 2022. The project uses 40 floating turbines supplied by Helix Turbines, which allow installation in deeper water where winds are stronger and visual impact from the shore is lower.

Construction began in 2023. The turbines are assembled at the Port Linden Marine Terminal and towed to the site. The project was partly financed by the Aurora Bay Green Bond and by private investment from Meridian Capital.

The Coastal Fishermen's Association raised concerns that the turbine array would disrupt fishing grounds. After mediation led by Marcus Oyelaran of the Office of Sustainability, Tidewater Energy agreed to a fisheries compensation fund and to leave navigation corridors open between turbine rows.

First power from Northwind was delivered in late 2025. When complete, it is expected to supply about 45 percent of Aurora Bay's electricity.

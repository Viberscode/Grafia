The 2019 Harbor Blackout

On 14 August 2019 a heat wave pushed electricity demand in Aurora Bay to a record peak. The aging Saltmarsh Gas Plant, owned by Aurora Bay Power and Light, tripped offline when its cooling system failed. Within an hour, cascading failures at two substations left 60,000 households without power for three days.

An independent review led by grid engineer Dr. Priya Raman of Coastal State University found that the city depended on a single fossil-fuel plant with no backup storage. The Raman Report recommended three actions: add large-scale battery storage, diversify generation with renewable sources, and modernise the substations.

The blackout became a turning point in local politics. Elena Vasquez, then a city councillor, made grid resilience the centre of her successful 2020 mayoral campaign. After taking office she cited the Raman Report directly when proposing the Aurora Bay Clean Energy Plan.

The Saltmarsh Gas Plant was scheduled for retirement in 2028, contingent on the Kestrel Battery Storage Project and the Northwind Offshore Wind Farm coming online.

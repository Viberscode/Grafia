Financing the Transition: The Aurora Bay Green Bond

In May 2022 the City of Aurora Bay issued the Aurora Bay Green Bond, a 400 million dollar municipal bond dedicated to clean energy infrastructure. The bond was underwritten by Meridian Capital and certified under the International Green Bond Standard.

Proceeds were allocated as follows: 180 million dollars to the Northwind Offshore Wind Farm transmission connection, 130 million dollars to the Kestrel Battery Storage Project, and 90 million dollars to the GreenHomes Retrofit Program.

City treasurer David Chen said the bond was oversubscribed three times, reflecting investor confidence in the Aurora Bay Clean Energy Plan. The bond was complemented by a 120 million dollar grant from the National Clean Grid Fund, a federal program administered by the Department of Energy Resilience.

The Aurora Bay Taxpayers Alliance, led by spokesperson Gordon Hale, argued that bond repayments would burden future budgets. In response, David Chen published an analysis showing that avoided fuel costs from retiring the Saltmarsh Gas Plant would cover most of the repayments.

Meridian Capital also made a separate private equity investment in Tidewater Energy to support Northwind construction.
